# lynox Sub-processors

The list of sub-processors that lynox AI engages for **lynox Managed Hosting** is published at <https://lynox.ai/subprocessors> (German: <https://lynox.ai/de/unterauftragsverarbeiter/>). The contractually binding list is section 9 of the Data Processing Agreement at <https://lynox.ai/dpa> (German: <https://lynox.ai/de/avv/>). This file keeps no copy of the list.

## Self-hosted lynox

The self-hosted software (`@lynox-ai/core` and `@lynox-ai/web-ui`) has no sub-processors of lynox AI: lynox AI receives no data from your installation unless you opt in to sending error reports to lynox (`LYNOX_BUGSINK_DSN`). What your installation connects to follows from your configuration and from what you ask the agent to do — the LLM provider you configure, a web-search backend in a default install, and any accounts, APIs and addresses you connect or ask the agent to fetch. Whether any of these is a sub-processor, a recipient or neither depends on your deployment and on your own role as controller.

## Changes and contact

lynox AI notifies Managed Hosting customers at least 30 days in advance of any addition or replacement of a sub-processor (DPA section 9). For questions, or to object to a change: <privacy@lynox.ai>
